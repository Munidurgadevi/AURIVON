01 Information We Collect

When you enquire about an AURIVON service or book a consultation, you may choose to provide information that helps us understand your project and contact you.

01

Contact Details

Your name, phone number, email address and preferred way to contact you.

02

Project Details

Your location, room type, requirements, preferred appointment time and installation goals.

03

Room Information

Measurements, layout details, photos or other information you choose to share for planning.

04

Communications

Messages, questions, feedback and information related to quotations, bookings or support.

02 How We Collect Information

We generally receive information directly from you when you:

  • Submit a contact, consultation or booking form.
  • Call, email or message our team.
  • Share room photographs, measurements or project preferences.
  • Discuss a quotation, installation, equipment setup or after-service support.

Basic technical information may also be processed by the hosting platform or your browser when you visit the website. The exact information depends on the services and settings in use.

03 How We Use Your Information

We use information for purposes connected with your enquiry or the service you request, including to:

  • Respond to questions and arrange consultations or site visits.
  • Understand your room, recommend suitable equipment and prepare quotations.
  • Plan and deliver installation, calibration and support services.
  • Communicate appointment updates or project-related information.
  • Maintain our records, protect our services and improve customer experience.

Our approach: We aim to use the information you provide for relevant AURIVON enquiries, planning, installation and support rather than unrelated purposes.

04 Room Photos and Project Details

Room photos, measurements and layout details can help us plan speaker placement, screen positioning, cable routes and other installation requirements.

Please share only information that is needed for your project. We will not treat private room photographs as permission to publish them in our portfolio or marketing materials. We will seek your permission before using identifiable project images publicly.

Helpful tip: Before sending photographs, check that they do not unintentionally include personal documents, screens or other private information you do not want to share.

05 Information Sharing

AURIVON does not sell your personal information. We may share relevant details only when reasonably needed to deliver a service you request, operate the website, or meet a legal obligation.

  • With technicians or project partners who need details to carry out your installation.
  • With service providers that support website hosting, communications or business operations, where applicable.
  • When disclosure is required by law or needed to protect rights, safety or security.

Third-party services may handle information under their own terms and privacy policies.

06 Data Security

We take reasonable care when handling information shared with us and aim to limit access to people who need it for the relevant work. However, no website, email service or electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Please use care when sending private information and contact us if you believe information you shared with AURIVON has been misused.

07 Data Retention

We keep enquiry, quotation, booking and project information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you, provide services and support, maintain business records, resolve disputes or meet applicable legal requirements.

How long information is retained may depend on the type of record and the reason it was collected.

08 Your Choices

You can contact us to ask about personal information you have shared with AURIVON, request a correction, or ask us to stop using it for a particular enquiry where appropriate and legally permitted.

  • Provide only the details needed for your enquiry or project.
  • Tell us if your contact or project information changes.
  • Let us know if you do not want project photographs considered for public display.

Some records may need to be retained where required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

Questions About Your Privacy?

Contact our team about this policy or information you have shared with AURIVON.
