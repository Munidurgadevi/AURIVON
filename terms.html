02 Our services

AURIVON plans, installs and tunes home theatre and audio systems for homes and businesses in Hyderabad and nearby areas. Depending on your project, this can include:

  • Home theatre design and room planning.
  • Surround sound and multi-room audio installation.
  • Projector and screen setup.
  • Acoustic treatment.
  • Calibration and final tuning.

Please note: descriptions, photos and examples on our website are for guidance. The final scope, equipment and layout for your room are confirmed in your written quotation.

03 Quotes & bookings

A site visit or consultation helps us prepare an itemised quotation for your room, seating and budget. A quotation is valid for the period stated on it.

A booking is confirmed only when you accept the quotation and we acknowledge it in writing. Installation dates are agreed in advance and may move if site access, equipment delivery or other circumstances outside our control change.

04 Payments

Prices, applicable taxes and the payment schedule are listed in your quotation. Some projects need an advance payment before equipment is ordered or work begins.

Equipment remains our property until the amount due for it has been paid in full. Delayed payments can delay the project schedule.

05 Your responsibilities

To complete your project smoothly, we ask you to:

  • Give our team safe access to the room and the areas where cables or equipment will be installed.
  • Share accurate details about the room, wiring, power points and any existing equipment.
  • Tell us about structural, electrical or interior work by other contractors that may affect the installation.
  • Make sure you have the right to approve work on the property, if you are not the owner.
  • Review and approve the final layout and equipment list before installation begins.

06 Warranty & support

Equipment is covered by the warranty of its manufacturer. Our installation workmanship is covered for the period stated in your quotation.

Warranty does not cover damage from misuse, power surges, liquid spills, unauthorised repairs or changes made to the system by anyone other than AURIVON. If something does not work as expected, contact us and we will arrange a visit.
