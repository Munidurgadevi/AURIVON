Our story

It started with one room that sounded wrong

AURIVON began when a simple living room setup looked impressive but never sounded right. We learned that great sound is not about the most expensive equipment. It is about the room, the placement and the care taken in tuning.

Today we bring that same attention to every project, from compact apartment lounges to dedicated cinema rooms across Hyderabad.

What we do

From first idea to final note

One team handles design, installation and tuning, so your system works as one.

  1. Home Theatre Design

    Layouts planned around your room, seating, screen size and budget, before a single wire is run.

  2. Surround Sound

    Balanced, immersive audio with precise speaker placement and clean cabling.

  3. Projector & Screen

    Sharp, bright pictures matched to your room, your lighting and your seating distance.

  4. Acoustic Treatment

    Panels and room treatment that tame echo, so dialogue stays clear and bass stays tight.

  5. Multi-Room Audio

    Music in every room, controlled from one simple app, without cluttering your interiors.

  6. Calibration & Tuning

    Final measurement and tuning so every seat in the room gets the best possible sound.
