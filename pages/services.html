Good to know

Questions, answered

Straight answers to what clients ask us most before they begin.

Get in touch
01 How are your prices decided?

"From" prices are starting points. After a site visit, we provide an itemised quote covering every speaker, cable, and labour cost before work begins.

02 Can you work in a room that is already finished?

Yes. We plan cable routes carefully so wires stay out of sight, even in painted and furnished rooms. We explain options during the visit.

03 Can you upgrade the system I already own?

Often, yes. We review what you have, keep the parts that still perform well and replace only the weak links, so you do not need to start from scratch.

04 How long does an installation take?

It depends on the scope, from a short visit to a longer schedule for a dedicated room. We agree on the date and plan before work starts.

05 What happens after the installation is done?

We test the system with your own movies and music before handover, then stay available for check-ups, re-calibration, updates and quick fixes.
