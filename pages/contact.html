Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Do you charge for the first visit?

No. The first consultation visit is free. We look at your room, listen to your needs and recommend a setup that fits your space, budget and lifestyle perfectly.

How long does an installation take?

A living room system is often done in one to two days. A dedicated cinema room takes longer, and we share a clear timeline after the visit.

Can you work with equipment I already own?

Yes. We can reuse speakers, screens or receivers you already have and suggest upgrades only where they make a real difference.

Do you offer support after installation?

Yes. We calibrate everything, walk you through the controls and stay available for aftercare support to ensure the best possible entertainment experience.
