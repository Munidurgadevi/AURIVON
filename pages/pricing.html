Good to know

Before you choose a package

Does the package price include equipment?

These are installation package starting prices. Equipment like speakers, AV receivers, projectors is quoted separately based on your preferred brands & specifications.

Can you create a custom package?

Yes. If your room needs a different combination of audio, video, acoustics or automation, we can build a personalized custom quotation tailored after the consultation.

How do you decide the final price?

We carefully consider room dimensions, existing wiring, equipment, mounting requirements, acoustic work, automation and the amount of professional calibration required.

Is a site visit required?

A site visit or detailed room consultation is recommended for accurate planning, especially for projector mounting, speaker placement and acoustic treatment.
