Home Theatres

Multi Room / Smart Audio

Music that follows you from room to room. Play one playlist everywhere, or a different song in the kitchen, bedroom and living room at the same time. Speakers stay neatly out of sight, and one app or wall panel lets everyone in the family control volume and sources without any fuss.

Commercial Sound Experiences

Our Services

What we offer

The finishing touches around the core installation that make a home theatre feel complete.

Cinema Lighting & Scenes

Dimmable cove lighting, starlight ceilings and preset scenes for movie, music and party modes, all changed with a single tap.

Soundproofing & Isolation

Keep the sound in and the noise out with insulated walls, doors and ceilings, so late-night movies never disturb the family or neighbours.

Streaming & Source Setup

Set-top boxes, streaming players, gaming consoles and Blu-ray players connected neatly, with one smart remote that effortlessly runs everything.

Gaming & Sports Rooms

Low-lag big screens and punchy sound, perfectly tuned for console gaming, cricket nights and exciting live match days with friends.

Upgrades & Retrofits

Already have a system? We improve what you own by replacing weak links and reworking the layout, without starting from scratch.

Support & Maintenance

Regular check-ups, re-calibration, software updates and quick fixes, so your system keeps performing like day one.
