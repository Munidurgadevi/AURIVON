Whole Home

Multi Room Audio

Music that follows you from room to room. Play one playlist everywhere, or a different song in the kitchen, bedroom and living room at the same time. Speakers stay neatly out of sight, and one app or wall panel lets everyone in the family control volume and sources without any fuss. From morning routines to evening gatherings, enjoy clear, balanced sound in every corner of your home. Commercial Audio

Good sound is part of how your business feels. For offices, showrooms, cafes, restaurants and event spaces, we plan audio that covers the room evenly, keeps announcements clear and lets background music set the right mood.
